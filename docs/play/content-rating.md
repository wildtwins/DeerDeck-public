# Content Rating And Audience Worksheet

## Recommended Answers

- App category: Lifestyle / field utility.
- Ads: None.
- In-app purchases: None.
- User-generated public content: None. User notes and media remain private/local unless the user exports them.
- Communication between users: None.
- Location sharing with other users: None.
- Violence, fear, gambling, sexual content, profanity, drugs, or controlled substances: None built into the app.
- Target audience: General audience, not designed specifically for children. Select age groups 13 and older unless the intended store strategy changes.
- News or educational claim: No.
- Health app: No.
- Government app: No.

## Permission Rationale

- Location: Optional field coordinates, sunset, weather, and environmental context.
- Camera: User-initiated photo and video capture.
- Microphone: User-initiated session audio notes. Speech-to-text at session close uses the device's configured recognition service.
- Internet: Optional National Weather Service requests.
- Storage/document access: User-selected media and backup destinations through Android platform APIs.

Answer the live IARC questionnaire from the final binary and listing. Do not infer a rating from this worksheet alone.
