## Full Description

Turn everyday wildlife watching into a reliable long-term field record.

Withrow DeerDeck opens directly to a fast observation grid, so logging a sighting takes one tap. Start with locked Wildlife or Birds profiles that adapt their regional defaults from GPS, or create as many fully customizable profiles as you need.

Capture the full context of an observation:

- Wildlife counts, descriptors, tags, and session notes
- Optional GPS coordinates and local sunset time
- Current weather, temperature, pressure, humidity, wind, and moon phase
- Photos, videos, voice notes, and speech-to-text notes
- Automatic session end after extended inactivity

See patterns over time with 30-day trends, wildlife share, similar-day comparisons, one-year-ago context, best-condition analysis, and searchable session history.

Your records are local-first and under your control. Create verified backups on your phone or in a Google Drive folder you choose. Export a complete ZIP with available media, share privacy-filtered records, and restore safely with a pre-restore recovery backup.

Choose light, dark, or system appearance. Use the location-aware Wildlife and Birds profiles, or create unlimited named profiles with custom wildlife, colors, and ordering.

No account. No ads. No analytics. Your wildlife journal stays yours.
