Withrow DeerDeck Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 25, 2026

Withrow DeerDeck is a local-first wildlife observation app. It does not require an account and does not include advertising, analytics, or a developer-operated cloud service.

Data stored on your device

The app stores observation sessions, wildlife counts, notes, tags, profile settings, environmental details, media references, and backup preferences on your device. Photos and videos are stored in your camera roll or app-specific storage according to your setting. Voice notes are stored locally. The app does not sell personal data.

Optional location and weather

If you enable field conditions and grant location permission, the app reads your approximate or precise location to record coordinates and calculate local context. The app also selects a broad regional Wildlife or Birds list locally on your device. Coordinates are sent over encrypted HTTPS to the United States National Weather Service at weather.gov to retrieve weather and forecast details and, when needed, to the USGS Elevation Point Query Service for elevation; profile selection does not create an additional network request. The developer does not receive or retain this network request. You can disable field conditions in Settings and use the core observation features without location access.

Camera, microphone, and speech input

Camera permission is used only when you choose to capture a photo or video. Audio recording is used only when you choose to create a voice note. Optional speech-to-text is handled by the speech recognition service configured on your device and may be subject to that provider's privacy terms.

Backups, exports, and sharing

Backups and exports occur only when enabled or initiated by you. A Google Drive backup uses Android's system document picker, which lets you choose a folder controlled by your Drive provider. The app does not receive your Google password or run its own Google account service. Exports and shares go only to the destination or app you select.

Security and retention

Network weather requests use HTTPS, and Android app storage protections apply to local data. Your data remains until you delete individual records, use Settings > Privacy & Data > Erase local data, clear app storage, or uninstall the app. Camera-roll media and independently exported backups are separate and must be removed from their destination by you.

Children

Withrow DeerDeck is a general-audience field utility and is not designed or directed specifically to children.

Changes and contact

This policy may be updated as the app changes. The effective date above identifies the current version. Questions or deletion support requests can be directed through https://github.com/wildtwins.
